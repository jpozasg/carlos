## Unit GEARBOX (level id = gearbox, or the variant when it has none)
Owns the gearbox's maintenance and faults (the gearbox row itself, `gbx.identity`, and `var.gearbox` belong to the VARIANT unit and exist already): `gbx.service` (`GEARBOX_OIL` job with interval and workshop price, or a check `NONE FOUND`), `gbx.clutch` (manual/DCT: `CLUTCH` job with typical life, DIY parts and workshop price, or a check), `gbx.risks` (faults, each priced, or a check `NONE FOUND`), `var.gearbox` (automatics: check of this gearbox's weak points), and the check `rel.GEARBOX.gearbox_specific` on the gearbox.
Start: `variant(<the listing's variant>)` (`listing({LISTING_ID})`), `todo(vehicle_id=<variant>)`. Write with `item(...)`, `fault(...)` and `check(...)` at the gearbox / gearbox family level (`level=` accepts it; re-scope a misplaced row with one PATCH rather than duplicating it), `job='GEARBOX_OIL' | 'CLUTCH'`. Protocol steps 4 and 5 (gearbox oil service, clutch, gearbox faults).
No browser. Last line: `CARLOS_RESULT done: <summary>` or `CARLOS_RESULT failed: <reason>`.
