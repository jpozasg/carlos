## Unit CAPTURE: read the listing and write it
Owns `lst.capture` and `lst.photos`. Browser: Claude in Chrome, a new tab of your own, closed at the end. No browser, or several connected and none selected: do not pick one, end `CARLOS_RESULT failed: browser unavailable (<what the tool said>)`.

1. Read `docs/browser-tasks.md`. If the URL is `carlos://listing/<id>` (or a listing of this account without a variant): `GET /api/listings/<id>`; if its ad is still online re-capture it; if offline, the reference car, or stored-only, do not fail: attach the variant with `PATCH /api/agent/listings/<id> {"vehicle_id": ...}` when the stored HSN/TSN allow it, log a `verification` entry ("ad offline / own car: researched from the stored capture") and end `CARLOS_RESULT listing_id=<id>`.
2. Otherwise read the page, write a capture JSON in your scratchpad, run `python3 capture_listing.py <capture.json> --dry-run`, then for real. It writes source, listing, capture, photos, checklist and the `found` log entry; a known HSN/TSN reuses the shared catalogue variant. Structured facts as the ad states them (null when not shown, never guessed): `seller_type` DEALER/PRIVATE, `warranty` 1/0, `hu_until` YYYY-MM, `service_history` YES/CLAIMED/NO, `accident_free` YES/CLAIMED/NO, `postal_code`, and for a dealer ad always `seller_rating` and `seller_reviews`. Electric car: the seller's battery-health figure goes in `battery_soh_claim`. Photos the script skips: fetch through the browser or report them.
3. **Out of scope (#165):** a new car (no first registration, delivery km only) or a lease-only ad is not captured: end `CARLOS_RESULT failed: out of scope: new car / lease-only ad (second-hand purchases only, #165)`. A used ad that also shows a lease rate is captured as a purchase.
4. Listing unreadable (blocked, login, CAPTCHA): stop and `CARLOS_RESULT failed: <reason>`.

Do no research beyond the capture. Last line: `CARLOS_RESULT listing_id=<the listing id>` or `CARLOS_RESULT failed: <reason>`.
