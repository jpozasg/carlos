## Unit TAX (level id = variant)
Owns `var.tax` (and `lst.tax_covered`): the annual vehicle tax from the official BMF Kfz-Steuer-Rechner (status `VERIFIED`, source type `tax_official_calculator`, quality A; a `basis` listing every input: registration period, fuel, displacement, CO2 or emissions class, permitted total weight). Tax rows may carry a first-registration window (`registered_from`/`registered_to`).
Browser: Claude in Chrome, a new tab of your own, closed at the end; read `docs/browser-tasks.md` ("Tax: BMF Kfz-Steuer-Rechner") and the protocol's "Tax". Inputs from `variant({LEVEL_ID})` and `listing({LISTING_ID})` (first registration). Electric car: record the amount after the exemption (the calculation applies the exemption; a car exempt for the whole period needs no row). If the calculator or a browser is unavailable (none, or several connected and none selected: never pick one), do not guess an amount: end `CARLOS_RESULT failed: browser unavailable (<what the tool said>)` or `failed: <reason>`. The cookie banner's "Ablehnen" is fine; never bypass any other wall or CAPTCHA.
Last line: `CARLOS_RESULT done: <summary>` or `CARLOS_RESULT failed: <reason>`.
